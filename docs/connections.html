Connect your stores

Debut needs permission to work inside your Apple and Google accounts. You only do this once, and we walk you through every click.

🔒
Your keys are stored encrypted and are never shown to the AI you chat with. Debut does the work on your behalf, and anything permanent (like submitting to Apple) always asks you first.

Apple App Store

Lets Debut build, upload to TestFlight, write your listing, and submit for review.

Not connected

What Debut will do with this

  • Send test versions of your app to your phone
  • Create your store listing, screenshots, and description
  • Set up subscriptions and in-app purchases
  • Submit for review and handle rejections
  1. Open your Apple developer account

    Sign in at App Store Connect → Integrations. This is Apple's website, not ours.

    Don't have an Apple Developer account yet? It costs $99/year from Apple and takes about a day to approve. Start here.
  2. Create a key for Debut

    Click the blue + button. Name it "Agent Debut" and choose the access level App Manager. Then click Generate.

  3. Download the file Apple gives you

    Apple gives you a file ending in .p8. You can only download it once, so keep it somewhere safe.

  4. Give it to Debut

    Copy the two IDs shown next to your new key, then drop the file below.

    📄

    Drop your .p8 file here or click to choose it

Takes about 3 minutes.

Google Play

Lets Debut upload Android builds, fill in your Play listing, and publish.

Not connected

What Debut will do with this

  • Upload your Android app to testing tracks
  • Write your Play Store listing and add screenshots
  • Set up Play Billing for subscriptions
  • Roll out releases to real users
  1. Open your Play Console

    Sign in at Google Play Console, then go to Setup → API access.

    No Play Console account yet? It's a one-time $25 fee from Google. Sign up here.
  2. Create a service account

    Click Create new service account and follow the link to Google Cloud. Name it "Agent Debut" and create a JSON key.

  3. Give it permission

    Back in Play Console, find the new account and grant it Admin (all permissions) for your app, then click Invite user.

  4. Give the file to Debut

    Drop the JSON file Google gave you below.

    📄

    Drop your JSON file here or click to choose it

Takes about 5 minutes.
