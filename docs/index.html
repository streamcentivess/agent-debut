Frequently asked

What are AI credits?

Credits are what your App Manager spends when it works. Pro includes 2,500 credits a month — plenty for normal shipping — and new organizations get 500 starter credits free for their first 30 days when they connect their App Store Connect account.

What if I run out of credits?

Nothing gets cut off mid-task. Enable usage-based billing and we top up your balance automatically with 2,000 credits for $15 whenever you run low, up to a monthly spend limit you control. You're never billed after the fact.

Can Jones really turn my Lovable or Emergent app into a mobile app?

Yes. Connect the GitHub repo your builder pushes to, and Jones wraps it in a native shell, flags and fixes what the stores reject (web checkout, missing account deletion, privacy policy), and ships it to TestFlight and the Play Console.

Do I need Apple and Google developer accounts?

Yes — Apple's Developer Program ($99/yr) for the App Store and a Google Play Console account ($25 one-time) for Android. Jones walks you through setting up both and handles everything inside them for you.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. There are no contracts and no setup fees, so you can cancel whenever you want.
