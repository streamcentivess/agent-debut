How it works

Three steps between your working tree and the App Store.

Connect

Add Smith's MCP endpoint to your agent with one command. It shows up as a set of tools your agent can call.

Delegate

Tell your agent "ship this to TestFlight" or "get 1.2 submitted." It hands the Apple work to Smith.

Ship

Smith reports back with build status, listing state, and review progress — until the app is live.

Smith MCP
